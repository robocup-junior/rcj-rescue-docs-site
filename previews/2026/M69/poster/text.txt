  Innovative mechanisms (Rescue Maze):

                                              CUSTOM DRIVE — BaseX + EV3
                                              No ready-made MicroPython driver exists for the BaseX motor base,
                                              so we wrote our own from its I2C register map (basex.py: straight, drive,
                                              encoder odometry).
                                              Gyro-PD heading control is layered on top.

                                                                                                                              • Verified the maze logic on desktop (grid visualiser) and a motors-disabled dry-run before hardware

                                              DIFFERENTIAL SUSPENSION
                                              A central differential lets the left and right sides flex independently,
                                              keeping all four wheels on the ground over speed bumps and
                                              uneven tiles — reliable traction and odometry

                                              SERVO RESCUE-KIT DROPPER
                                              Two micro-servos deploy rescue kits to the left / right wall
                                              when a victim is detected.
