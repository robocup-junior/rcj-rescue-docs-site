In their words

Our robot was entirely designed and built by the team to compete in the RoboCup Junior Rescue Maze category. The mechanical structure was conceived as a three-floor independent modular architecture, allowing quick hardware modifications and component replacements between rounds, giving the team a significant competitive advantage in adaptability.

To handle speedbumps up to 3 cm, the robot features a custom-designed suspension system that ensures stable ground contact across uneven terrain, maintaining sensor accuracy and locomotion consistency throughout the course.

Navigation is powered by a custom mapping algorithm based on Dijkstra's shortest path method, enabling the robot to efficiently explore and traverse the maze while continuously updating its internal map in real time. This approach allows fast and reliable pathfinding even in previously unknown environments.

The robot's electronics are built around a fully custom Printed Circuit Board (PCB), designed to be versatile and easily reconfigurable to meet different competition requirements. Vision capabilities are provided by a pair of OpenMV RT1062 cameras, capable of processing images at high frame rates and running embedded AI models directly on the hardware, enabling real-time victim detection without relying on external processing.

Together, these systems make our robot a robust, adaptable, and intelligent platform, engineered from the ground up for competitive performance.
