In their words

The robot is designed to compete in the RoboCupJunior Rescue Line. We have been working for almost three years to create a robot capable of overcoming all the challenges presented by the competition. Over time, the robot has undergone numerous modifications: we have added artificial intelligence systems; we have increased the number of sensors; we have used better and more suitable sensors for our goals and we have furthermore improved our mechanical components by the addition of a brand new tire system built from scratch. Thanks to these improvements, the robot has significantly enhanced its performance, demonstrating greater reliability in handling complex line-following sections and improved capability in overcoming physically challenging elements of the course. The entire robot was designed in 3D using Fusion 360.

To achieve greater reliability, we decided to rely only on one microcontroller, getting rid of the old serial communication system. In fact, all the computations are done by a Raspberry Pi 5, programmed in Python, connected to a Pi camera, which is used to identify and follow the line, using the tools provided by the OpenCV library, additionally the camera is used to manage the evacuation zone, recognizing the entrance and the exit of it. The Raspberry is also connected to an AI hat, using a model trained with TensorFlow Lite, it is able to recognize victims and evacuation points in the evacuation zone using a second vertical Pi camera.
